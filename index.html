Before you order下单须知

The practical details matter.把实际细节先说清楚。

Price & availability价格与库存

Illustrations are references, not live inventory. Current item photos, availability, specifications, currency, and total price must be confirmed in chat.页面插画仅作参考，并非实时库存。当前实拍、库存、规格、币种和总价须通过聊天确认。

Shipping, duties & timing运输、税费与时效

Shipping options and estimates depend on destination. Import duties or taxes may be charged by the destination and are not assumed to be included unless confirmed.运输方式和预计时效取决于目的地。除非另有确认，目的地产生的进口税费不视为已包含。

Returns & issues退换与异常

Return, cancellation, damage, and sizing terms vary by order. Ask for the applicable terms and confirm them in writing before payment.退换、取消、破损和尺码相关条件可能因订单而异。付款前请索取并以文字确认适用条款。

Privacy & payment safety隐私与付款安全

This site has no checkout form and does not collect card details. Contact occurs on third-party messaging platforms under their policies. Never share a password, recovery code, or one-time verification code.本站没有在线结账表单，也不收集银行卡信息。联系通过第三方通讯平台进行并受其政策约束。不要分享密码、恢复码或一次性验证码。
