FAQ

Common questions

How long does shipping take?

In-stock items are packed within 1–2 days. Delivery time depends on your location and the courier option you choose — we always share a tracking number.

How do I pick the right size?

Send us your usual size (EU/US/CN) and foot length in cm. We'll recommend the best fit for each specific style, since fits can vary.

Can I see real photos before ordering?

Yes — message us the style you're interested in and we'll send real product photos and details before you commit.

What if there's a problem with my order?

Contact us within 7 days of receiving your item. We'll help with returns or exchanges for any quality or sizing issue.
